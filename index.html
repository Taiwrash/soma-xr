How to use Soma XR

  1. Seated Mixed Reality (Quest 3 / 3S): select Enter MR with controllers set down. Everything works seated within a two-foot radius.
  2. Hands-first console: tap floating buttons directly with your index finger, or aim and pinch from your seat.
  3. Tabletop 3D & Slicing: tap Tabletop to bring a compact 3D body within effortless reach, sweep Finger lens, or tilt an open hand in Palm slice.
  4. 14 Languages & Voice: switch languages anytime or select the microphone to ask questions aloud.
